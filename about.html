Welcome to Fortunes High School

Fortunes High School, located in the heart of Hyderabad near Tilak Nagar, is a trusted name in quality education. Recognized by the Government of Telangana, we provide a strong academic foundation for students from Nursery to 10th class.

As one of the best schools in Hyderabad, we focus on holistic development by combining academics, life skills, and values. Our school creates a safe, engaging, and inspiring environment where every child is encouraged to learn, explore, and grow with confidence.
